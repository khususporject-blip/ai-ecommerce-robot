Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Robot AI ("we", "our", or "the app") provides an AI-assisted shopping experience. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Robot AI and its TikTok sign-in integration.

Information we receive

When you choose to sign in with TikTok, Robot AI may receive the profile information permitted by the authorization screen, such as a TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar. We do not request information that is not needed for the account experience.

How we use information

We use account information to authenticate your account, maintain your session, personalize the application experience, and provide requested features.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only when needed to operate the application or comply with law.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Authentication credentials and tokens are intended to be handled on the server side and are not displayed to other users. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law.

Your choices

You can stop using Robot AI and request deletion of account-related information by contacting the app operator through the support channel made available in the application.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Robot AI operator through the support contact associated with this application.
